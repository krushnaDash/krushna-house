# First-Floor Plan – Kutpali House

One 1 BHK + one 2 BHK on the existing pillar grid, with a door that can be opened to make one 3 BHK.

- **Drawing:** `drawio/first-floor-plan.drawio` – page 1 "1 BHK + 2 BHK", page 2 "3 BHK (merged)". PNG previews sit next to it.
- **Status:** design brief v1, written before drawing. Items marked ⚠ are my assumptions – change any of them and I will redraw.

## 1. Software

draw.io (diagrams.net), the only design tool installed and the same one as `rooftop-pillar-layout.drawio`.

- Drawn 1 ft = 24 px, so pillars land on exactly the same coordinates as the pillar file.
- Pillars are locked. Walls are in the same layer, so they snap to pillar faces.
- Furniture, dimensions and notes are separate layers you can hide.
- For permit or structural drawings an architect can redraw it in AutoCAD/Revit using the pillar table.

## 2. Fixed inputs (not changed)

- Roof 48′-0″ × 30′-0″ and 12 pillars of 10″ × 10″ (A1–D3), at the positions in `rooftop-pillar-layout.drawio`.
- 4′-0″ extensions from the pillar faces to the slab edge: left (A1–A3), front (A3–D3), back above C1–D1.

## 3. Zoning

| Zone | Use | Why |

Every pillar sits inside a wall, so no column stands in the middle of a room. A2 and B2 sit in a 9″ wall on the A2–B2 row.

## 5. Making it a 3 BHK

- **Connecting door:** 3′-0″ in the party wall, centred in the C2–C3 gap, between the two living rooms.
  - Page 1 shows it locked. Fit a bolt or lock on each side, so both owners control their side.
  - Page 2 shows it open.
- **What each space becomes when merged:**

| 1 BHK space | In the 3 BHK |
|---|---|
| Living | Family lounge |
| Bedroom | Bedroom 3 |
| Bath | Bath 3 |
| Kitchen | Pantry / utility (keep the plumbing and hob points) |
| Front door | Secondary entrance, kept locked |
| 2 BHK front door | Main entrance |

- **Merged total:** ≈ 1,010 sq ft + balcony.
- **Services:** keep two electricity meters and two water lines at the gallery wall. That lets each unit run on its own, and the 3 BHK can use one and leave the other idle.

## 6. Rules applied

- Walls are 10″ on external and party lines, flush with the pillar faces, and 4½″ for partitions.
- Every habitable room has a window on an external wall.
- The common bath opens off the passage, never through a bedroom.
- Entrance doors swing inward, because the gallery is only 4′ deep.
- Windows sit between pillars, never in them.
- Bath doors do not swing into fixtures.

## 7. Flags for the structural engineer

- The left and back strips are 4′ cantilevers. The west wall, Bath 1 and Bath 2 sit on the left one, and the balcony parapet on the back one. These need a load check, a sunken slab and waterproofing in the baths. A lighter wall such as 6″ AAC on the cantilever tip is an option.
- The front gallery is also a 4′ cantilever, so check its parapet (3′-6″ minimum, per local code) and live load.
- Slab and beam layout and the floor height are not given. That affects partitions on the slab and the bath sinks.
- Check local bye-laws for highway setback, minimum room sizes, ventilation and the single-stair egress distance.

## 8. Assumptions to confirm ⚠

1. The stair is outside the slab and lands at the east end of the front gallery (shown dashed, position to be confirmed).
2. The back strip is a balcony. Alternatives are to enclose it for +64 sq ft or to put a bath there.
3. The split is on the C line: 2 BHK west, 1 BHK east. Splitting on the B line instead gives a 1 BHK of 408 sq ft and shrinks the 2 BHK to about 636 sq ft.
4. No compass direction is given, so no Vastu rules are applied.
5. Furniture is indicative only.
